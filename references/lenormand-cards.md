## Combination Methodology

Lenormand is read as **sentences**, not individual cards. Each adjacent pair forms a grammatical unit: the first card is the **subject (noun)**, the second card is the **modifier (adjective/verb/adverb)**.

### The Noun + Modifier Model

```
Card A (Subject)  +  Card B (Modifier)  =  Combined Meaning
     ↓                      ↓                      ↓
  The core theme     How it is changed     The blended reading
```

**Key rule**: The order of cards matters. A+B is different from B+A.

- **Rider (1) + Clover (2)** = "Lucky news arriving" — news (Rider) is modified by luck (Clover)
- **Clover (2) + Rider (1)** = "A fleeting visitor" — a small opportunity (Clover) takes the form of a visitor/arrival (Rider)

### How to Use Modifier Types

Each card has a `modifier_type` — a label describing how it typically modifies the card to its left when it appears as the second card in a pair.

When interpreting Card A + Card B:

1. Start with Card A's `core` meaning (the subject)
2. Apply Card B's `modifier_type` to understand HOW B changes A
3. Blend using Card B's `core` meaning for the specific flavor
4. Check if Card B is in the negative modifier list — if so, the pair carries a darker tone regardless of Card A's polarity

### The 7 Negative Modifiers

These cards darken or add difficulty to ANY card they touch — even positive ones. When one of these appears as the second card in a pair, the tone shifts negative regardless of the first card:

| Card | Darkening Effect |
|------|-----------------|
| **Clouds (6)** | Obscures meaning, adds confusion and doubt |
| **Snake (7)** | Adds hidden complications, jealousy, or deception |
| **Coffin (8)** | Signals endings, stagnation, or loss |
| **Scythe (10)** | Introduces sudden cuts, danger, or abrupt change |
| **Whip (11)** | Adds conflict, repetition, or stress |
| **Mountain (21)** | Blocks, delays, or creates obstacles |
| **Mice (23)** | Erodes, diminishes, or causes gradual loss |
| **Cross (36)** | Adds burden, suffering, or karmic weight |

**Example**: Bouquet (9) + Mountain (21) = "Joy blocked" or "Happiness delayed." Bouquet is a positive card, but Mountain's blocking effect dominates the pair.

### The 5 Positive Amplifiers

These cards brighten or enhance whatever they touch:

| Card | Brightening Effect |
|------|-------------------|
| **Sun (31)** | Brings success, vitality, and warmth |
| **Stars (16)** | Adds hope, clarity, and inspiration |
| **Key (33)** | Unlocks solutions and confirms certainty |
| **Bouquet (9)** | Beautifies, brings gifts and pleasure |
| **Clover (2)** | Lightens, adds luck and optimism |

## Timing Reference

Timing attributes describe the general pace at which a card's energy manifests. These are approximate and modulated by surrounding cards:

### Fast (days to ~2 weeks)
Rider (1), Clover (2), Bouquet (9), Scythe (10), Birds (12)

### Medium (weeks to ~2 months)
Ship (3), Stork (17), Letter (27), Moon (32), Key (33)

### Slow (months to a year+)
House (4), Tree (5), Mountain (21), Lily (30), Anchor (35)

### Context-Dependent
Clouds (6), Snake (7), Coffin (8), Whip (11), Child (13), Fox (14), Bear (15), Stars (16), Dog (18), Tower (19), Garden (20), Crossroads (22), Mice (23), Heart (24), Ring (25), Book (26), Man (28), Woman (29), Sun (31), Fish (34), Cross (36)

**Practical rule**: When spread cards include timing cards, lean toward the indicated pace. When a context-dependent card dominates the spread center, use the question's timeframe as the default.

## Spread Interpretation Quick Reference

### Card Count by Spread

| Spread | Cards | Key Reading Strategy |
|--------|-------|---------------------|
| 1-Card Yes/No | 1 card + verdict | Apply card's core meaning to question; state verdict clearly |
| 2-Card | 2 cards | Read 1+2 as single combination |
| 3-Card / Daily | 3 cards | Chain: 1+2, then 2+3; center card (2) is core |
| 5-Card | 5 cards | Chain: 1+2, 2+3, 3+4, 4+5; center (3) is core theme |
| 9-Card Box (3×3) | 9 cards | Three perspectives: time (columns), layers (rows), cross method (center + arms + corners) |
| A-or-B Choice | 3+3 cards | Chain-read A (1+2, 2+3) and B (1+2, 2+3) separately; compare trajectories |

### Interpretation Checklist

For every reading, the agent should:

1. **Identify all adjacent pairs** — every card touches its neighbors; no card reads in isolation
2. **Apply modifier types** — the second card in each pair modifies the first according to its modifier type
3. **Check for negative modifiers** — if a pair contains a negative modifier as the second card, the tone shifts darker
4. **Synthesize into narrative** — chain the pairs into a connected story, not a list of bullet points
5. **Center on the core** — in odd-numbered spreads, the center card is the thematic anchor
6. **End with practical takeaway** — what should the querent know or do?
