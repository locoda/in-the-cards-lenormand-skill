# Lenormand Card Database — 36 Cards

Full deck reference for interpretation. Each card entry includes traditional meanings, polarity, and combination modifier notes. Cards are rendered as text blocks with polarity-colored backgrounds — no external images.

## Card Index

### 1. Rider · 騎士
- **Slug**: `rider`
- **Suit**: 9 ♥ (Nine of Hearts)
- **Polarity**: neutral
- **Keywords (EN)**: news, arrival, visitor, message, movement, swiftness, delivery
- **Keywords (ZH)**: 消息、到來、訪客、訊息、移動、迅速、傳遞
- **Traditional meaning**: A messenger bringing news or a visitor arriving. Signals momentum, new developments, or someone entering the querent's life. The Rider accelerates whatever it touches — paired with positive cards, good news; with negative cards, trouble approaching quickly.
- **Combination modifier**: Speeds up or delivers the meaning of adjacent cards. Rider + Clover = a lucky break arriving soon. Rider + Coffin = bad news or an ending approaching.

### 2. Clover · 幸運草
- **Slug**: `clover`
- **Suit**: 6 ♦ (Six of Diamonds)
- **Polarity**: positive
- **Keywords (EN)**: luck, opportunity, lightness, short-term joy, chance, optimism
- **Keywords (ZH)**: 幸運、機會、輕鬆、短暫的快樂、機遇、樂觀

It brings lightness and optimism but warns that the favorable moment is fleeting. Paired with negative cards, it softens the blow; with positive cards, it amplifies cheer.
- **Combination modifier**: Lightens heavy cards, adds a spark of hope. Clover + Mountain = a small break in a long difficulty.

### 7. Snake · 蛇

## Combination Reading Principles

1. **Adjacency is everything** — cards modify their neighbors. A card's meaning shifts based on what sits beside it.
2. **Read left to right** — the flow of the spread moves from past toward future, from cause to effect.
3. **Noun + Adjective model** — the first card is the subject, the second modifies it. Rider + Clover = "lucky news"; Clover + Rider = "a fleeting visitor."
4. **Chain pairs** — Card 1+2, then 2+3, then 3+4... Each overlapping pair tells part of the story.
5. **Center dominance** — in odd-numbered spreads, the center card is the core theme.
6. **Do not cherry-pick** — every card matters. A difficult card surrounded by positive ones tells a different story than a difficult card surrounded by negative ones.
